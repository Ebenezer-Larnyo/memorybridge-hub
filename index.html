PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE

Words you may hear

Dementia
An umbrella term for symptoms that affect memory, thinking and everyday function. Alzheimer’s disease is the most common cause.
Respite care
Short-term care that gives a caregiver a break, at home, in a day program or during a short stay.
Adult day program
Supervised daytime activities and care outside the home.
Memory care
Residential care designed for people living with memory loss.
Palliative care
Care focused on comfort and quality of life for serious illness. It can be given alongside other treatment.
Hospice
Comfort-focused care for people near the end of life, with support for their families.
Power of attorney
A legal document that lets a chosen person make decisions for someone else. Rules differ by place, so ask a qualified adviser.

General explanations only, not medical or legal advice.

Resource quality

Privacy and safety

No accounts, analytics scripts or personal medical information are requested. Checklist storage is optional and local.
