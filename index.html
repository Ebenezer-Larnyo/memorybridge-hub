Built from research

Inspired by Larnyo, Benyassine and Preece, Using Social Media Posts to Understand the Health Information Needs for Older Adults with Dementia: A Data Mining and Machine Learning Approach, section 5.11. This prototype is not clinically validated.

Resource quality

The crawler discovers links from selected public sources. New links are not human reviewed. A functioning link does not guarantee clinical reliability.

Privacy and safety

No accounts, analytics scripts or personal medical information are requested. Checklist storage is optional and local. Browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser provider only after you choose to use it. Hosting providers and external sites have their own privacy policies. This is information, not diagnosis or treatment. Seek a clinician for medical decisions. In an emergency contact local emergency services.
